How to use Chronoglobe

  • Drag the big slider to travel through time. It is stretched so recent centuries get more room. Use the ‹1 / 10› / 100» buttons or ← → (hold Shift for 10 years) for precise steps, or click the year to type one in.
  • The thin slider under it picks a month and day (far left = the whole year). Once you pick a date, events earlier in the year are lit and ones still to come are dimmed.
  • Click any territory to read what was happening there at that moment, see events inside it and find links to learn more.
  • Click an icon to read about that event. Faded icons happened in the years around the selected year.
  • 🗞️ World now lists everything happening around the world in the current period, grouped by region.
  • Search for an empire, a battle or a person's event — e.g. “Mongol”, “Inca”, “Mali”, “Hastings”.

Where the data comes from

Borders from 3400 BC on: Cliopatria, Seshat Global History Databank (CC BY 4.0; coordinates simplified for the web) — over 1,600 states with borders year by year. Earlier maps and the faint layer of peoples without a state: historical-basemaps by André Ourednik (GPL-3.0). All historical borders are approximate. Curated events and territory summaries were written for this app from the Wikipedia articles cited (numbered) at the end of each text, and checked against them. Extra events and territory descriptions are loaded live from Wikidata and Wikipedia (CC BY-SA).
